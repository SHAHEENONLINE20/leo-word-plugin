Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the LEO Plugin ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Microsoft Word add-in.

Information Collection

LEO analyzes text content from your active Word document using Natural Language Processing (NLP) to provide readability and comprehension scores. We do not collect or store any document data on our servers unless explicitly permitted by the user.

Data Usage

All data processing is done in-memory or temporarily cached (if needed) for performance via Redis. No document content is stored permanently. Usage statistics may be anonymized to improve our models and services.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Azure or Replit for hosting and analytics. These services comply with relevant data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR).

Security

We employ secure connections (HTTPS) and follow best practices for authentication and data handling. User content is never used for training models or shared externally.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@leoplugin.site
